# Reproducibility

Paper: 64 queries, DocFormerV2 (344M) without visual branch, frozen LLaVA-OneVision/InternVL2/Qwen2-VL, two-stage 140K+100K training. Local: 8-query, 1-layer 64-wide modules and synthetic invoices on CPU/MPS. Paper reports DocVQA ANLS improvements such as InternVL2 56.0 to 86.6 at 448x448; no local result is comparable. Hardware/dataset/model-scale differences make a reproduction claim invalid.
